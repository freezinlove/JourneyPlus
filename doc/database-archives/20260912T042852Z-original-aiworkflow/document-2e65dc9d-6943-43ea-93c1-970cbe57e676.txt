Flying Blue Status Match Terms & Conditions

These specific Flying Blue Status Match terms and conditions apply in addition to the general StatusMatch.com Terms of Service. 
 

* Only 1 status match per person will be permitted under this campaign matching to Platinum, Gold or Silver tiers.
* The application fees vary by region, tier and eligibility. The fees will be displayed to you prior to any purchase commitment, and are available below as a guide.
* Only residents of target countries that hold locally government issued identification are permitted to apply.
* If approved, the Status Match upgrade is valid for 12 months. To retain your status past this date, you must re-qualify based on meeting the published status programme requirements on flyingblue.com.
* All Status Match requests are handled by and are the responsibility of StatusMatch.com and not by Flying Blue or any of its affiliates.
* In addition to other eligibility requirements, a Status Match will only be granted to applicants whose name matches the records in the Flying Blue program.
* With the participation in the campaign, participants acknowledge that the data submitted to StatusMatch.com may be shared with Flying Blue. [Read the full privacy policy here](../)
* Only elite status holders of eligible airlines, who pay the relevant application fee and are approved, will receive the matched status.
* The Status Match offer is limited in number of matches and time frame at the sole discretion of StatusMatch.com and Flying Blue. It may end at any time.
* Members that are approved for a Status Match will not be eligible for any potential subsequent Status Match with Flying Blue.
* The awarded Flying Blue status may be suspended, revoked or terminated by Flying Blue in accordance with the Flying Blue program terms and conditions.
* Flying Blue reserves the right to deny the status match approval for members who have violated the Flying Blue program terms and conditions in the past.
* Flying Blue may take up to 5 business days after approval by StatusMatch.com before the upgraded tier is visible in the member's account.
* Approved members will not receive a physical status card.
